Why a checkpoint?

  
When AI steps pass work along, the person's own words may not reach the step that makes the final recommendation. In our recorded pilot, the final step got the previous AI step's output but not the person's priority as a separate instruction. It doesn't show that a reminder always works, or that the choices were better.

What this brief can and can't do
    
    
  • It makes your priorities explicit, so you can see whether the answer uses them and notice when it doesn't.
    • 
    
  • It can't ensure that an AI follows it, and it doesn't make a decision better. Sometimes you'll want the AI to question your priorities; the brief asks it to do that openly, so you can decide.
    • 
    
  • Nothing you type is saved, sent or analysed. It stays on this page until you close or reload it.
