What you can recompute, and what you cannot
From the public files
Every Study 3B count, outcome classification and paired sensitivity on this site; the re-parsing of every final choice from its visible first line; the value-name indicator; that Delegated and Restored received the identical T6 and that T4 re-anchored shares T1–T3; Study 2's tallies; Study 1's matched estimate from the scores.
Only with the private records
Study 3B and Study 2: that each text is byte-for-byte what the API returned, and the hash-chained ledger that orders every call. These records include the model's thinking summaries and request metadata, so they are not published. Study 3B's record hashes are in study3b_summary.json under provenance, so a future audit of the private records can be checked against them.
Study 1: that the displayed texts match the saved corpus they were exported from (the corpus contains the withheld priority wording, so it is not published). The SHA-256 of each corpus file is recorded with the site's build. Study 1 was not run with a call-by-call ledger.
Not available at all
The questionnaire-derived priority wording used in Study 1 (withheld until its reuse is cleared), and any model thinking text.
Code and reproducibility materials

The analysis and build code (study protocols, frozen analyses, the independent recomputation, the site builders and the acceptance tests) is prepared for an open-source release, together with a reproduction script for the Study 1 estimate. It will be linked here once the public repository is created.
