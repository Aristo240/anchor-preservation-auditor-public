Inspect and compare a test run

Open a result from priority_check chain or priority_check workflow. The page answers five questions about it, shows what was measured and what wasn't, lets you read every recorded handoff, and compares it with a later run.

Which workflow was tested: priority_check chain runs our fixed six-handoff reference workflow (advisor → critic → summarizer, twice) on your decision. It does not run your own agents. A result here tells you how that reference workflow behaved with the model you chose. It is not an evaluation of your own multi-agent system. Your Workflow Map is a description of your system's structure, not a test of it. priority_check workflow is different: it runs your own system through an adapter you write. Each result below says which of the two it came from.

  1. 1 · Map where the priority is given: Workflow Map
  2. 2 · Test your own workflow with priority_check workflow (adapter guide), or our reference workflow with priority_check chain; save a scenario first
  3. 3 · Inspect and compare the results

Open a result
