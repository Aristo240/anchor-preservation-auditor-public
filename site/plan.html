Three questions about your workflow

Answer for the step that makes, or recommends, the final choice. "Not sure" is a useful answer: it becomes something to find out. Your plan is built from these answers; this page can't see your system.

1 of 3When the final choice is made, does that step receive the user's own statement of what matters to them?

For example, "keeping the cost low matters more to me than speed", in the user's words or a faithful copy, not only a summary written by an earlier AI step.

2 of 3Do you record which option the final step actually chooses?

Not just whether its answer mentions the user's priority, but which option it picks.

3 of 3Have you tested whether the choice switches when the user's priority is reversed?

For example, "speed matters more to me than cost" instead of "cost matters more than speed".

How will you run the tests?

The tests only tell you about your workflow if you run them through your own final step. Running the prompts in a general chat assistant shows how the method works, but says nothing about a different system.
