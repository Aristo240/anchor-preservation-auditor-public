Research · Study 4

Study 4: does the final decision still follow the priority after delegation? (Gemini 2.5 Pro)

What was measured

Outcomes per condition

Each decision is coded MATCHED (chose the option its priority favours), OPPOSITE, UNCLEAR (declined), INVALID (no answer in the required form) or MISSING (no usable response, including a failed earlier handoff). They are never merged. A unit is one scenario, option order and chain: both of its opposing priorities decided.

Pair classes per condition

Compared with no priority at all

The same scenarios decided with no priority stated. This comparison is descriptive: it shows similar behaviour, not why the decisions changed.

Primary estimates (descriptive)

Prespecified sensitivity analyses

Interpretation limits

  • One model (Gemini 2.5 Pro) and 12 constructed scenarios from one template family, written and reviewed by one person with prior knowledge of the hypotheses.
  • About 12 scenario clusters: percentile intervals can undercover and are descriptive; no significance tests.
  • What the handoff texts contained was not analysed, so this is not evidence that the priority was literally forgotten.
  • Restating the priority shows the model follows an instruction placed in front of it, not that its decisions were better.
  • A controlled experiment: it does not say how often this happens in other models, workflows or real use.
