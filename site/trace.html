Review a workflow trace

Check a recorded workflow of your own in four steps: the decision, the priority, the handoffs and the final choice. Everything stays in this browser tab.

What this does: shows your handoffs side by side, highlights words you choose (literal matching only), and compares the final choice with the option you say matches the priority. What it does not do: run any AI model, extract values, classify text, or judge whether a departure is a problem. One pasted run supports inspection; it is not a controlled experiment, because nothing was varied. To test whether a decision changes with the priority, see the test step. Nothing is uploaded, stored or kept after you close or reload the page, unless you export it.
